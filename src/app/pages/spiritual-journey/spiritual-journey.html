Faith Before Every Beginning

A Life Guided By Devotion

Every day begins with prayer. Long before the first meeting, the first classroom, or the first decision of the day, there is a quiet moment of stillness — a discipline unbroken across three decades of building institutions that now touch hundreds of thousands of lives.

Every month begins with the blessings of Lord Jagannath. On the first day, without exception, the journey leads back to Puri — a pause to seek grace before the weight of a new month begins.

Faith has always inspired every mission of education, compassion and humanity.

Lord Jagannath

At the centre of this devotion stands Lord Jagannath — a presence that has shaped not just personal faith, but a philosophy of service itself. The deity's message of unity, welcoming people of every background without distinction, mirrors the very mission that KIIT and KISS were built upon.

A Temple Within the Campus

Within the KISS campus stands its own Jagannath Temple, in what is known as Sreevani Khetra — built so that the children who call KISS home never have to look far for faith. Every year on Diwali, following the rituals of the Puri Srimandir, over a thousand earthen diyas are lit around the deities of Lord Jagannath, Lord Balabhadra and Devi Subhadra — a tradition observed without fail, year after year.

Monthly Pilgrimage

On the first day of every month, the journey leads to the Shree Jagannath Temple in Puri. It is not ceremony for its own sake — it is an act of humility, a pause to seek blessings before the responsibilities of a new month begin.

Rath Yatra at KISS — A Tradition of Faith

At the heart of this celebration is Dr. Achyuta Samanta, whose devotion to Lord Jagannath has made the Rath Yatra an enduring part of life at KISS. Year after year, he performs the sacred Chhera Pahanra — the ceremonial sweeping of the chariots — before thousands of devotees begin the journey. What began as a celebration of faith has grown into a powerful expression of community, tradition and service.

Faith Through Service

In the end, every prayer finds its true expression not inside a temple, but outside it — in a classroom built for a child who had none, in a meal served to a student who needed it, in a life quietly changed. Faith gives direction. Service gives it purpose.
